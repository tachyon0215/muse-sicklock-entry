# muse-sicklock-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Locked In by My Own Lock](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36).

## The entry

> Can't come in today - my smart lock updated overnight, decided my face is 'suspicious activity', and has locked me in with my own groceries.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A fresh, half-believable excuse (a smart-lock update flagging its owner's face) that reads like a real message, wastes no words, and lands its twist at the end (locked in with the groceries); avoids the worn fever/dog/grandmother excuses the brief warns against.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 bb9aeffc2e850c8e86700f7cf805695ab68b454f5e3ce5f9da3ca78460971838).
